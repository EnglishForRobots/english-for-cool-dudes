2Grammar — Circle the Correct Option
Choose the sentence that is grammatically correct.
1. Third Conditional — what might have happened, but didn't:
A. If Griffin spent more time in Ireland, he wins the case.
B. If Griffin had spent more time in Ireland, he might have won.
2. Subject–verb agreement:
A. The tribunal judges say the ruling is final.
B. The tribunal judges says the ruling is final.
3. Reported speech (a past claim about an earlier time):
A. Griffin claimed that he lives in Ireland for most of his life.
B. Griffin claimed that he had lived in Ireland for most of his life.
4. Preposition after "accused":
A. Griffin was accused of hiding his true tax home.
B. Griffin was accused for hiding his true tax home.
